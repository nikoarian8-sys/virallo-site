How it works

One upload a month. A full feed of short videos back.

01

Get your shot list

Each month we send a simple list of clips to film on your phone: your space, your products, your people.

02

Drop the raw clips

Upload everything to a shared folder. No editing, no captions, no stress about getting it perfect.

03

We edit and write

Hooks, cuts, captions, on-brand graphics and trending audio, planned around your local calendar.

04

You approve, we post

Review the month in one link. Nothing goes live without your OK. We schedule it to Instagram and TikTok.

Questions

Where is your team based?

Jakarta, Indonesia. We work Australian business hours, and your account lead is available by email, WhatsApp or video call.

Do I need to film anything?

Yes, but only raw clips on your phone. Our monthly shot list tells you exactly what to capture. We handle everything after that.

Is there a contract?

No lock-in. Every package is month-to-month, and you can change volume or pause with notice before your next cycle.

Can you just edit, and we post ourselves?

Yes. We can deliver finished videos to your folder and let your team schedule them.

Do you work with agencies?

We do. We offer white-label short-form production for Australian agencies. Email us for agency rates.
